    The three questions everyone asks

    What bag can I bring?

    A clear one. Plastic, vinyl or PVC, no bigger than 12 × 6 × 12 inches. A one-gallon clear freezer bag also works. Backpacks and regular purses stay in the car.

    When should I park?

    Lots generally open five hours before kickoff, and the ones closest to the gates fill first. Buy your parking pass before game day so you know which lot is yours.

    Can I tailgate?

    Yes. Tailgating runs from the time the lots open until two hours after the game, in the grass areas along the edges of the lots.
