About the Game Day Guide

A small, independent help desk for one very specific day: going to a Dallas Cowboys home game at AT&T Stadium.

Why this exists

A first trip to AT&T Stadium comes with a surprising amount of homework. Which bag gets through security? When do the lots open? Can you grill in the parking lot? The answers exist, but they are spread across policy pages, parking maps and fan forums, and most people go looking for them in the car on the way there.

Game Day Guide puts those answers in one place and lets you ask for them in plain language. The goal is simple: nobody should miss kickoff because of a backpack or a parking lot.

What you get

  • A chatbot that knows game day. Ask it about the bag policy, parking, tailgating, when gates open, what you can bring and accessibility. It is on every page, in the bottom corner.
  • A game day clock. Tell it the kickoff time, how you like to arrive and how long your drive is, and it works out when to leave home, park and head to your gate.
  • This walkthrough. The whole day in order, for people who would rather read than ask.

Who it is for

First-time visitors, fans traveling in from out of town, families bringing kids, and whoever in the group chat got stuck organizing the tailgate. Season ticket holders already know all of this. Everyone else is who this guide was written for.

A home game, start to finish

  1. Before you leave

    Put your tickets and parking pass on your phone, and pack a clear bag. Approved bags are clear plastic, vinyl or PVC and no larger than 12 × 6 × 12 inches. A one-gallon clear freezer bag counts.

  2. Parking

    Lots generally open five hours before kickoff, and closer to four hours for noon games. The stadium has 15 numbered lots, with more parking in the lettered lots around the ballpark next door.

  3. Tailgating

    You can tailgate from the moment the lots open until two hours after the game ends. Set up in the grass areas along the edges of the lots and keep the driving lanes clear.

  4. Getting in

    Stadium gates open two hours before kickoff. Every bag is inspected, so have yours open and your mobile ticket on screen when you reach the front of the line. Lines are longest in the final hour.

  5. Accessibility

    Accessible parking is first come, first served, and shuttles run from the lots to the stadium for guests with limited mobility. Ask the chatbot for details, or contact stadium guest services before your visit.

  6. After the final whistle

    Thousands of cars leave at once, so the lots clear slowly. Because tailgating is allowed for two hours after the game, plenty of fans fire the grill back up and wait out the traffic.

How it was built

This site is a class project. The chatbot was built with Chatling, the video on the home page was made with an AI video generator, the pages were coded with an AI assistant, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Game Day Guide is not affiliated with the Dallas Cowboys, AT&T Stadium or the NFL. Policies and opening times change from game to game, so check attstadium.com before you go.
